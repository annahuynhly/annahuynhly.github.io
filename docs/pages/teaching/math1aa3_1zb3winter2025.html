Calculus for Science II & Engineering Mathematics II · McMaster University · Winter 2025

About These Notes

Tutorial notes written by Anna Ly for MATH 1AA3 (Calculus For Science II) and MATH 1ZB3 (Engineering Mathematics II) during the Winter 2025 semester. Despite the different course codes, the course content is the same.

We followed Calculus by Stewart et al., 9th edition. Tutorials focused on questions uploaded on Childsmath.

Note: some section titles in the documents do not exactly match the titles listed here, as one section ran earlier than the other and content was shifted to match due dates.
